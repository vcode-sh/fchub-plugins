Connect your AI app to your FluentCart store for safe, read-only answers about its data. Refunds,
subscription cancellation, deletion, and bulk actions are unavailable in every mode. Order-status
changes, marking an order paid, and other money-moving work are also unavailable.

## New in 2.1.0: FluentCart 1.6 subscriptions, without the drama

You can now list renewals and open an individual renewal directly, then see the richer subscription
context FluentCart 1.6 returns. Reversible mode also has a narrowly guarded update for a
store-billed subscription's billing-cycle limit (`bill_times`), with a just-in-time preflight and
read-back. It accepts only `manual` or `system` collection methods; gateway-billed
`automatic` subscriptions are not edited.

The server cannot charge a card, create, skip, void, resend, or cancel a renewal. Those buttons
are still where they belong: behind a deliberate human decision.

**Compatibility note:** “FluentCart 1.6+” is this release's compatibility theme, not a promise
about untested future versions. The exact verified combination is WordPress 7.0.2, FluentCart Core
1.6.0, and FluentCart Pro 1.6.0. FluentCart Pro is optional; route discovery simply shows fewer
Pro-backed tools when it is not installed.

## Safe by default

The default is read-only. Refunds, subscription cancellation, deletion, bulk actions, order-status
changes, and marking an order paid are unavailable here. Mercifully, nobody has delegated the cash
register to a chat box.

## Ask these first

> "Show me the FluentCart dashboard stats."

> "Show me all orders from the last 7 days."

> "Which products sold best this month?"

> "What renewals are due next week, and which subscriptions are paused?"
