Connect your AI app to your FluentCart store for safe, read-only answers about its data. Refunds,
subscription cancellation, deletion, and bulk actions are unavailable in every mode. Order-status
changes, marking an order paid, and other money-moving work are also unavailable.

## New in 2.2.0: MCP SDK v2, without the interpretive networking

I rebuilt the protocol layer on the official MCP TypeScript SDK v2 packages and pinned each one to
`2.0.0`. I support both MCP `2025-11-25` and `2026-07-28`, including modern `server/discover`,
cache hints, and real negotiated-handshake evidence over HTTP and STDIO.

I also added a live registry gate, official conformance coverage across both protocol versions,
and exact-candidate client, Docker, proxy, archive, and soak evidence. Dependency drift may still
have ambitions. I no longer give it a release strategy.

I retained the FluentCart 1.6 subscription support from 2.1.0. I still cannot charge a card,
create, skip, void, resend, or cancel a renewal. Those buttons remain where they belong: behind a
deliberate human decision.

## Safe by default

The default is read-only. Refunds, subscription cancellation, deletion, bulk actions, order-status
changes, and marking an order paid are unavailable here. Mercifully, nobody has delegated the cash
register to a chat box.

## Ask these first

> "Show me the FluentCart dashboard stats."

> "Show me all orders from the last 7 days."

> "Which products sold best this month?"

> "What renewals are due next week, and which subscriptions are paused?"
