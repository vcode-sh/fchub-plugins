The dashboard tells you what needs attention today. **Memberships > Reports**
helps explain what is changing over time and where the membership offer deserves
a closer look.

Choose a date range once and move between the report views without rebuilding
the same question in a spreadsheet.

<Cards>

    trend across the selected period.
  </Card>

  </Card>

  </Card>

## Reading the numbers

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Why does a new site have empty charts?">

Trend, retention, and cohort views need history. The dashboard becomes useful
immediately, while time-based reports fill in as daily snapshots accumulate.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where does revenue come from?">

Membership revenue follows the FluentCart orders connected to membership
access and uses the store's configured currency presentation. FluentCart
remains the detailed source for full commerce and subscription accounting.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is churn always bad?">

No. A fixed-term programme ending exactly when promised is not the same problem
as a monthly plan losing members after one cycle. Use plan, period, renewal, and
retention views together before declaring an emergency in the group chat.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I retrieve report data externally?">

Yes. Administrative report routes are documented in the
[Developer Reference](/docs/fchub-memberships/developer-reference#rest-api).

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Useful next actions

- Open expiring members from the dashboard and decide whether they need a
  renewal message.
- Compare trials with paid conversions before changing an offer.
- Use FluentCRM segments for targeted renewal or win-back journeys.
- Review plan distribution before retiring or combining plans.
- Treat revenue as a membership view, not a replacement for FluentCart
  accounting.
