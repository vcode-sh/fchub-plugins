## No active roadmap

FCHub Stream is discontinued, with maintenance suspended indefinitely. There are no planned features, compatibility updates, support commitments, or scheduled releases.

The previous roadmap mentioned upload limits, Bunny.net webhook changes, and video analytics. Those were ideas, not commitments, and they are not being developed.

## Continuing independently

The source remains available under GPLv2. Anyone may fork it, rename it where required, and continue in their own direction. Please use your own repository for issues, releases, and support.

The original project may return someday, but there is no roadmap or schedule.
